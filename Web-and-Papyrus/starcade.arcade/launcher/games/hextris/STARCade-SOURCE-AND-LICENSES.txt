Starcade changes remove advertising, analytics, score beacons, remote scripts,
remote fonts, store links, and social sharing. starcade-bridge.js connects local
score, finish, and input behavior to Starcade. These changes are distributed
under GNU GPL version 3 or later.

Bundled third-party JavaScript and fonts retain their own notices and licenses.
Their embedded headers/license metadata must be preserved. Complete editable
corresponding source is this directory and is also retained in the Starcade
Complete Source archive.
