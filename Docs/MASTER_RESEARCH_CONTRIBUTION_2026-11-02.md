Separate default/variant records, bounds and revision IDs; verify XP handling rather than inflating rewards. Research all launch/exit/controller/save paths. Acceptance: default exactly preserved, variant clearly labeled, no mixed leaderboard. Priority: near-term candidate.

Research bounded local save representation, finish/cancel recovery, native availability and plain-data restoration; no assumed world-engine effects. Acceptance: complete a small run, explain outcome, resume/abandon safely, no real inventory mutation unless separately designed. Priority: later original game, one lifecycle at a time.
4. **Holo-sports manager and playable sphere sport.** Consolidates existing #336, not competing football/baseball/NBA career systems. Presentation provider owns texture transport; producer owns gameplay/data, exactly one writer. Ordinary overlay remains independent fallback. Priority: research, not promised capability.
