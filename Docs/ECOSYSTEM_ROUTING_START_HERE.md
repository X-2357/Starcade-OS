**295 ideas have a proposed primary home; 58 remain deferred or require a separate scope.** A routed idea is not a promise to build it, a count of missing features, or proof that its original scope belongs wholesale in that mod. Related suggestions may collapse into one feature. Some candidates are high-risk research and some may already exist. The primary home identifies the responsible reviewing chat; supporting integrations stay with their owners.

The recent 30 overlap earlier ideas and are not 30 independently new approved mods. R01–R30 retain their recent-list identity; #1–#353 retain the original IDs. Do not renumber them together.

## Accepted new consolidation

- Corporate Wars: companies, operations, agreements and influence; SSSE retains market/financial authority.
- Shadow Network: Lost Signals, intelligence, informants and investigations share one case/evidence system.
- Legacy: captain's logbook, keepsakes, commemorations and historical presentation. Infamous retains live vessel/fame statistics and CT retains career authority.
- Rivals are already accepted in Infamous Ships. Company rivals belong in Corporate Wars; investigation antagonists in Shadow Network. No standalone universal Rivals mod.

## Shared interaction example

A company sponsors a supported colony facility. OC controls the local service; Corporate Wars owns the agreement; CT owns qualifications; LSS performs eligible local routines; Infamous handles a named shipping vessel. Shadow Network can investigate an authored incident. SSSE interprets an eligible public consequence. Legacy records actual milestones and AISS discusses appropriately disclosed facts. SSaW supplies campaign context, rescue owns survivor handling, Shore Leave controls leave permission, Discovery Journal owns exploration records and Starcade owns its game scores. Every adapter is optional and must be verified.
